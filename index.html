Diploma thesis

University of the Peloponnese · Supervisor: Athanasios Koutras, Assistant Professor

EEG-Based Control of an Open-Source Anthropomorphic Robotic Hand — the InMoov i2

A brain–computer interface that turns motor imagery into commands for a robotic hand: eight EEG channels, common spatial patterns with a linear classifier, and an Arduino driving the five servos of an InMoov i2 that I printed and assembled. Evaluation holds a whole run out of training, which is the scheme that matches real use.
